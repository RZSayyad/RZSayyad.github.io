About

My story so far.

I grew up in Dammam, Saudi Arabia, where I spent thirteen years at the International Indian School building a foundation in math, science, and the curiosity that eventually led me to write my first lines of code.

In 2022 I moved to Halifax to study Applied Computer Science at Dalhousie University, and the years since have been about turning that curiosity into shipped software. From robotics projects to a cross-platform app delivered solo for a real client, I've focused on owning the full lifecycle — design, code, deploy.

I work most comfortably where mobile, web, and data infrastructure intersect: REST APIs, real-time systems, data pipelines, CI/CD. I care about clean architecture and code that's still readable six months later. Graduating December 2026, looking for what's next.
